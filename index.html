All four lists are computed from verified inspections only — records still pending verification are excluded so no establishment is ranked on an unconfirmed reading — and cover establishments currently open. Trend uses a disclosed severity score of 3×Priority + 2×Priority Foundation + 1×Core, averaged per inspection, comparing the earlier half of an establishment's inspections against the more recent half (minimum four). An establishment can appear on two lists: a poor overall record that is improving sharply belongs on both.

How to read these records

Priority (P)
Violations most directly linked to foodborne illness — temperature control, contamination, employee illness.
Priority Foundation (Pf)
Missing procedures, records, training or equipment that support a priority item.
Core (C)
General sanitation, maintenance and facility upkeep.
Corrected on site
The inspector recorded the problem as fixed before leaving.
Repeat
Cited on a previous inspection and found again.
Pending verification
Every violation read off a form is checked against the count the inspector wrote in the form's own summary box. These records did not reconcile, or the summary box could not be read, so the reading is unconfirmed and not presented as fact.
No summary box
The inspector left the form's summary box blank, or the scan destroyed it, so there is nothing to check the reading against. The violations are shown as marked on the form, with a note.

Source. Inspection reports and the licensed-establishment list obtained from the Town of Greenwich Department of Health under the Connecticut Freedom of Information Act (Conn. Gen. Stat. §§ 1-200 et seq.). Inspections are recorded on Connecticut DPH form EHS-108 and cover the record period.

Please read. This is a reproduction of public records, not an official rating and not a health department grade. The town licenses all food service, so this list includes school, club and hotel kitchens, markets and delis alongside restaurants. Establishments marked closed appear in the inspection records but are not on the town's July 2026 licensed list; their addresses are not included in that list. Every reading is cross-checked against the count the inspector wrote on the form itself: most reconcile, some forms have no summary box to check against, and a few do not reconcile and should not be relied on. An inspection describes one moment in time. For the authoritative record, contact the Greenwich Department of Health.
